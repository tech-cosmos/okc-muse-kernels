## What's on main (submission.py)
M <= 32 (decode, bs8): 3 launches, `moe_tk2<NRT, NC=8, NSTG=8, GATED>` x2 + `moe_norm2`:
- 1 CTA/SM, 8 consumer warps + 1 producer lane. Work = (active expert, 8-token group, row-tile pair rp)
  "rows" R; CTA b takes rows [nrp*b/grid, nrp*(b+1)/grid). Per rp the producer streams 8 blocks; block q =
  8 units (k chunks q*8..q*8+7) = 3 cp.async.bulk copies (gate tile 4 KB, up tile 4 KB, scales 1 KB) into an
  8-stage smem ring (mbarrier full/empty; empty count = 256: every consumer lane arrives).
- Consumer warp w does k chunk q*8+w of every block, so its x fragments (8 kc x 2 uint4) stay in registers
  for a whole segment. Per rp: smem cross-warp reduce (double-buffered s_red, 1 named barrier), epilogue:
  gemm13 -> bf16 silu(gate)*up into inter[pos]; gemm2 -> FP32 y[pos] (plain stores, no atomics/zeroing).
- gemm13 CTAs compute the routing themselves (deterministic: __match_any_sync rank + per-warp counts), CTA 0
  writes meta/sroutes/inv for gemm2 + norm2. Experts with > 8 tokens: one segment per 8-token group (weights
  re-read per group). Scales repacked per pair: S2 [e][rp][kc][lane] u32 (_pair_scales in prepare).
M > 32 (TW_CFG = 218): route -> moe_gather -> moe_tw gemm13 -> moe_tw gemm2 -> moe_norm (5 launches).
- `moe_tw<RT=2, KG=1, NSTG=8, GATED>`: 384 threads = 2 consumer warpgroups + producer warp (setmaxnreg 40/232),
  1 CTA/SM. Item = (active expert, 64-token pass, row block of 8 warps x RT 16-row tiles; gated: per warp 1 gate +
  matching up tile), work-stealing counter meta[66]/[67] (zeroed by moe_route), heaviest experts first.
  Stage (1 k-chunk of 64) = TMA tensor-map copy of the x tile (box 64 k x {8,16,32,64} rows by nv, SW128, 1024-B
  aligned) + 1 bulk copy of the item's weights (prepare: _tb_pack -> w13b2/w2b2 [e][rb][kc][warp][RT][lane]) +
  1 of scales. Consumers: dq() into registers = wgmma RS A fragment (same layout as mma.sync A), wgmma
  m64nNk16 (N = 8*ceil(nv/8), 8 template variants, generated by _gen_wgmma), one group per k16 step, ring of 2,
  wait_group 1. Epilogue: gated -> silu*up bf16 into inter (K-PERMUTED column order, gemm2's B); else bf16 y[route].
- moe_gather: xg[pos] = x[sroutes[pos]>>3] with each 64-chunk permuted to the _pack_weight K order. xg / inter
  are per-call torch.empty; tensor maps are encoded inside moe_tw_launch from the passed buffer.
- ptxas budgets the whole 384-thread kernel at 168 regs even with setmaxnreg: anything more (RT=4, 2 k-steps per
  group, A ring of 4) gets "C7512 wgmma serialized" or DEPBAR 0. Check `-Xptxas -v` for C7512 and the SASS for
  `DEPBAR.LE gsb0, 0x1` (must not be 0x0 in the loop).
- Old big path still in the file: moe_big (TW_CFG = 0), moe_tb (mma.sync TMA, TB_RT).
- dequant dq(): o0 = q&M, o1/o2 = rotl(q,10/13)&M, o3 = ((q&0x001B001B)*16648)&M, M=0x81C081C0; 4 x
  bf16x2 mul with LUT scale. 12 instr per 8 weights. (_POS in prepare must match.)
- Also in the file (not used by default): moe_sk (register-ring stream-K + atomics, SK_CFG path when
  TK2_NSTG=0), moe_tk (TMA + atomics), moe_gemm (old decode), route2/act. Clean up when convenient.

## Ranked ideas
1. Big-M compute is the limit (ncu bs256: compute-only 180/100 us vs memory-only 166/87 vs full 210/118,
   issue ~55%, 2 consumer warps/SMSP latency-bound). Next: a 256-thread moe_tw (2 consumer WGs, producer duty in
   one consumer lane with non-blocking empty checks) so ptxas gets ~255 regs: then RT=4 (halves x traffic and
   per-k-step overhead), 2-step groups or a deeper A ring without serialization.
2. Fuse moe_gather into moe_route (multi-CTA route: deterministic ranking) or into gemm13's producer: -1 launch
   (~3.5 us on bs128/256). Also moe_norm (~7-10 us at bs256).
3. Decode bs32 (1.51x) is near memory-bound in the proxy (DRAM 83% at ncu clocks). bs16 1.87x: check DRAM%.
   moe_tw on decode loses (proxy bs8 91 vs 86, bs16 123 vs 90, bs32 149 vs 131).
4. (s5 done/dead for decode) PDL. Maybe: one persistent decode kernel (gemm13 -> grid barrier -> gemm2) only
   if a cheap way to avoid the routing recompute; low priority.
6. Decode experts with > 8 tokens (bs32 skewed routings): share dequant across 2 groups (n16 mma) with x of the
   2nd group in smem or registers (1 CTA/SM allows ~224 regs); L2-reorder (blk) is dead.
5. Clean-up: remove moe_sk / moe_tk / moe_gemm / route2 / act / moe_tb / moe_big from submission.py (~55 s compile).
